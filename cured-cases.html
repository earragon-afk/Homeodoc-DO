I give her a high dilution of the remedy corresponding to the whole picture of her symptoms and to the fact that she is going through menopause. Within ten minutes, the symptoms have disappeared. She only needs to take the remedy again two days later, when the same symptoms begin to return.

Woman, 57
She complains of persistent irregular heartbeats diagnosed as arrhythmia and moderate tachycardia. Her palpitations trouble her greatly because they are accompanied by breathing difficulties and sharp pains around the heart, radiating toward her left shoulder blade.

Woman, 63
Following chemotherapy for breast cancer, this woman develops severe breathing difficulties, which are diagnosed as asthma and for which corticosteroid inhalers are prescribed. However, the inhalers prove ineffective. When she comes to see me, she can no longer take her usual walks on the mountain without becoming so short of breath that she has to stop, and she then feels intense tightness across her chest. The cancer medication is known to cause symptoms of heart failure. Given the ineffectiveness of the inhalers, I conclude that the problem is more cardiorespiratory in nature, and I prescribe the remedy characteristic of her condition, to be taken every morning and evening. The episodes of breathlessness gradually become less frequent, and the tightness around the heart and chest stops completely and quickly. The improvement continues despite the ongoing chemotherapy treatment, which is scheduled to last five years.

Blocked ear

A musician came to see me about a blocked ear. She had a concert coming up very soon, at which she had to play her instrument, and this symptom was causing her considerable difficulty. During the consultation, I noted her physical symptoms, but together we were also able to identify what had happened just before her ear became blocked. She had experienced a very unfair situation but had not dared express her anger. That same day, her ear became blocked. Despite visits to specialists, no physiological cause could be found. Looking at her case as a whole revealed that she was very sensitive to injustices of this kind, even when they did not affect her personally. That very evening, after taking the homeopathic remedy, her ear finally cleared. This case once again illustrates the importance of emotional causes preceding the appearance of symptoms.

If she did too much in one day, she often ended up spending the weekend in bed, utterly exhausted. Together, we looked for difficult events that had occurred just before her pain began. A particularly difficult situation had arisen at work, where a colleague had caused her considerable worry. Combining this emotional cause with her particular symptoms, fears and a recurring dream, and discussing the things to which she was especially sensitive, I selected a homeopathic remedy that covered her case. Not only did the fibromyalgia disappear, but her troubled sleep improved, the recurring dream stopped, she was no longer afraid of snakes—even on television—and she could easily digest foods that had previously caused real problems. With a well-chosen homeopathic remedy that treats the whole person and the cause of the illness, a person can regain balance and health.

Eczema

Baby, eight months old
The eczema began in the third month of life, starting as patches of dry skin that gradually worsened into itchy red patches. The baby became irritable and woke frequently for no apparent reason other than visible discomfort. On questioning, it emerged that the eczema had appeared following a significant event experienced by the mother while breastfeeding. Cortisone creams were only moderately effective but necessary during severe flare-ups; otherwise, the mother managed the condition with oatmeal creams after bathing. In this case, the remedy made the eczema disappear within a few weeks. A constitutional remedy was then given to strengthen the child’s general health.

Man, 27
His eczema mainly affects his feet. It first appeared in adolescence after he slept outdoors while hiking. The soles of his feet began to burn, and small, fine blisters multiplied within a few days. Since then, the eczema has spread to his ankles and varies considerably from one period to another, often worsening in summer.

In general, this man easily feels too hot. He uncovers himself a great deal at night, particularly his feet, which become burning hot. He has loose, foul-smelling stools. He tires easily during the morning, with episodes of low blood sugar that he compensates for by eating chocolate bars around eleven o’clock. He also craves meat and spicy food. His sleep is restless and very light; he sleeps on his stomach and snores loudly for his age. This is a clear case in which a patient’s sensitivity has been awakened by a particular event. By combining his general characteristics with the signs of his illness, a classical remedy was found that completely freed him of his problem in less than a year.

Man, 75
His eczema developed on his legs, then his thighs and buttocks, without any obvious cause. Over the years, it worsened into attacks of itching when he undressed in the evening, continuing through the night until his skin was covered in scratch wounds and his life had become a nightmare. Creams proved unfortunate: although they helped temporarily, cortisone creams weakened the skin so much that lesions developed, requiring antibiotic creams. Following these treatments, the patient seemed to lose weight and become very vulnerable to respiratory problems. He was then skeletal, extremely sensitive to cold, exhausted from lack of sleep, and intensely irritable and intolerant because of the itching. His history was not encouraging: he had been treated for tuberculosis three times between adolescence and the age of forty-five.

This is a more complex case, summarized very briefly here. More subtle elements must be sought so as not to become overwhelmed by the many episodes of illness and all the signs arising from them. The remedy may be harder to find and may require several unsuccessful attempts. In this case, the first remedies enabled the patient to stop using his creams without too much itching, regain a little weight—although thinness is often hard to change at that age—and consequently feel less cold. But when the right remedy was found, the lesions began to disappear gradually, first on the buttocks, then the thighs and finally the legs. This gentleman, now eighty-five, still occasionally has itchy ankles, but the episodes are rare and there are no lesions. His sleep recovered long ago. His respiratory vulnerability remains, however, and he needs occasional follow-up when he becomes ill.

Psoriasis

A man presented with psoriasis on the glans of the penis, forming a sometimes painful crust. Curiously, this was the only place it had developed. Corticosteroid creams were not very effective, and the patient feared their long-term effects. In general, he was very nervous, restless and intensely stressed about time. He always arrived very early for appointments, but once there he was calm, even if I was running late. He told me that he did not start work until about five in the afternoon, yet was anxious from the moment he woke and could accomplish little during the day because he thought only about not being late for work. If he had a morning appointment, he would have trouble sleeping the night before. He had intense sugar cravings and could easily develop diarrhea under stress.

This case illustrates a sensitivity well known in homeopathy. The combination of physical and psychological signs pointed to a remedy similar to this patient, which brought about an impressive recovery. Within twenty-four hours of taking it, he had the mental impression that everything around him was slowing down, as though he himself had begun moving very quickly. Life then returned to normal, except that his anxiety about being late was greatly reduced and he arrived less early for appointments. Within a few days, a hard, leathery crust formed on the glans and quickly fell off, leaving pink, smooth but very sensitive skin.

Another case involved scalp psoriasis appearing as scattered, small, raised, itchy crusts. Its origin was impossible to identify: the patient could not remember when it had started because it had developed very gradually and she had treated it as dandruff. Only much later had a dermatologist diagnosed it and prescribed tar shampoos, which helped for a while before becoming less and less useful. She had also suffered from migraines since primary school, especially under stress. She was afraid of her teachers and could not tolerate arguments. Strong smells, such as perfume, could also trigger migraines. She had pronounced cravings for ice cream and spicy and salty foods, and could not tolerate eggs or fish. She was often thirsty for cold drinks. Most striking was her extraordinary emotional sensitivity. A violent television program showing people suffering could make her ill with a fever. She could not bear discussions of operations or injuries and was always ready to help anyone unfortunate, including animals.

Here we see a very different type of sensitivity that defines a very specific homeopathic remedy. There was nothing unusual about the psoriasis itself, but the migraines were revealing to a trained homeopath. Add the typical food preferences and characteristic emotional sensitivity, and there was no doubt about the remedy. It acted very effectively, first on the migraines, which disappeared after the first dose and only rarely returned as headaches when she slept too little. Over the course of consultations, she stopped complaining of itching, to the point that she had forgotten she ever had psoriasis. She remained sensitive but was less easily devastated by the outside world. She said, “It is as though there is now a little more distance between me and reality, so I no longer feel so strongly that I am experiencing what others feel when they are in pain.”

A farmer came to see me at least ten years ago with psoriasis so severe that he could hardly kneel to work in his field. He had eruptions from head to foot, but his knees were particularly affected, as though thick mushrooms had grown on them: the psoriasis was that thick. Kneeling had become so painful that a friend, a former patient, persuaded him to try homeopathy and urged him to consult me. It was difficult: he was a man of few words. He disliked discussing his emotions and maintained that the psoriasis must have followed an ankle injury after a stay at the beach, because that was where the eruption had first appeared. That had been a very long time ago. He had so few distinctive signs—what could be done?

At that moment, the friend who had accompanied him knocked on the door. Knowing him well, she asked whether it might help if she joined the consultation. What a relief! “Yes, of course,” he said. She asked whether he had mentioned a very unusual symptom. It was so distinctive that only one remedy could fit. After the first dose, he returned a month later as planned. His psoriasis had improved so much that hardly any eruption remained on his body. His knees were at least eighty percent better, and he could finally kneel in his field to work. It took about four doses in all, increasing the dilution once, for the psoriasis to disappear completely without returning.

Learning

School-age girl, seven
She is painfully shy, does not look at people she does not know, and has learning difficulties serious enough that she tells her parents she does not understand well at school and everything moves too quickly. Her teacher notes that she cannot keep up and is rapidly falling behind the rest of the class.

These difficulties had not previously been noticed because she had been cared for in a family setting, where she adapted well but did not have to undertake particularly demanding learning. She is also extremely meticulous: her drawings must be perfect, and she quickly becomes discouraged if she makes a mistake.

She also has an extreme difficulty making choices, even simple ones such as an item of clothing or a gift in a shop. She then becomes very agitated, irritable and insecure.

These characteristic elements enabled me to find a remedy that transformed this child over the following months. She now keeps up normally at school, although her pace is still a little slow. She can approach strangers in public places such as shops and restaurants, and her indecision has decreased so much that she now chooses her own clothes in the morning. Small mistakes no longer paralyze her to the same extent.

Fears in a young child

About three years ago, I saw an eight-year-old child who frequently complained of stomachaches. His parents no longer knew what to do. I stayed alone with the child so that he could speak freely without embarrassment. It is remarkable what children can reveal that nobody at home has suspected. He said his stomach did not hurt at all. He was afraid of the nasty, evil monster waiting for him whenever he was alone upstairs, alone in the basement, or alone in his bedroom at bedtime.

“It is a really nasty, evil monster! I know it is: it talks to me and tells me to do nasty things. I try not to listen, but it is always there waiting for me. I am really scared!” He had tried to tell his parents, but they played down his fear, saying that monsters did not exist. That was how the “stomachache” began, as a way to avoid being alone with the monster. I gave him the appropriate remedy, and the “stomachache”—the monster—disappeared. His parents were delighted: he no longer complained of stomachaches, went to bed easily, and began enjoying helping them, including going to the basement when asked.

Other cases

Bruxism (teeth grinding)

A mother consulted me about her four-year-old child’s severe bruxism. He ground his teeth not only at night but regularly during the day. It was clearly audible even during the consultation. As soon as he played on the floor or drew at the small table in the corner, he began grinding very loudly. His mother said, “His dentist is really worried. He says we must find a solution before he loses his baby teeth, because they are so worn down there is almost nothing left. We need a solution before his adult teeth come in, otherwise he will wear them all down in the same way and have no teeth left.”

When taking a homeopathic case history, one never knows where the distinctive signs needed to choose the right remedy will be found. I took the child’s history in detail. I learned that he had a difficult temperament and was often dissatisfied. He liked sleeping on his stomach with his knees tucked under him, as many young children do; beyond eighteen months, however, this becomes a particular symptom for us homeopaths. He had intense night sweats that soaked his pyjamas.

I got up to look at his teeth. Indeed, little remained of them. Remarkably, all their upper surfaces were concave. Interesting for the homeopath—a useful symptom. I asked whether either parent also ground their teeth. “Not me,” said his mother, “but my husband certainly does!” Did she know whether either paternal grandparent had done so? She did not. I asked her to telephone her mother-in-law. We learned that the child’s deceased paternal grandfather had also ground his teeth extensively. His grandmother explained that in photographs her husband had very small teeth because he had ground them throughout his life.

For me, the hereditary pattern and the distinctive shape of the teeth confirmed a history of syphilis in the family. I gave the child the appropriate remedy. When I saw mother and child a month later, she explained that from the second day after the dose, he had never ground his teeth again, either by day or by night. His night sweats had stopped, and he no longer slept in the same position. Given the hereditary aspect, I suggested that his father also take the remedy. He did, and he too stopped grinding his teeth. Years later, I saw the mother about a skin symptom: her child had never resumed grinding his teeth.
